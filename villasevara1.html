Villa Sevara is an exceptional six-bedroom retreat set above Camps Bay Beach, moments from the promenade and roughly a 15-minute drive from the Table Mountain Cableway. Every bedroom is air-conditioned, with load-shedding backup power and housekeeping Monday to Saturday.
